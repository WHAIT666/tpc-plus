# ADR-016: MVP subjects are Maths and History & Geography of Portugal

**Status:** Accepted · 2026-11-20

## Context

Plan v2.1 chose Maths (mode A) and Natural Sciences (modes B + C) for the MVP, before any discovery. M0 brought evidence: maths is where she struggles most (school report and teacher's comment); her HGP result is weaker than Natural Sciences; she ranks both maths and HGP among her least-liked subjects.

## Decision

MVP subjects: **Maths (mode A)** and **HGP (modes B + C)**. Natural Sciences moves to M7, alongside Portuguese and English.

## Alternatives considered

- **Keep Natural Sciences** — chosen without evidence; she does better there.

## Consequences

No architecture change: HGP is textbook text, the same as Natural Sciences. Maps and timelines may stress OCR; the M4 eval must include HGP pages with them. The first maths skills are fractions, confirmed against her corrected tests.
